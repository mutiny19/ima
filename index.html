First radio to first message

MeshCore is the text mesh most people in Indiana use. These four steps are everything a new user needs; the links go to the official MeshCore docs for anything deeper.

Get a radio

Any device on the MeshCore flasher list works. A small tracker with a built-in battery is the easiest start. Buy the US 915 MHz version.

Not sure what to buy? The MeshCore FAQ covers what you need to start, or ask on Discord before you order.

Put the Companion firmware on it

Open the MeshCore flasher in Chrome or Edge on a computer, plug the radio in by USB, pick your device, and choose Companion (Bluetooth).

Install the app and pick the USA preset

Get the MeshCore app for iPhone, Android, or the web. Connect over Bluetooth, give yourself a name, then in the radio settings choose the USA preset. It should read:

Frequency910.525 MHz
Bandwidth62.5 kHz
SpreadingSF7
Coding rateCR5

New radios often arrive on a European setting. If you hear nothing, this is the first thing to check.

These values come from MeshCore's own radio presets list, which is what the app shows. If the two ever differ, the app is right.

Say hello

  • Send a flood advert so nearby people and repeaters learn you're there.
  • Post in Public, or add the #test channel and send a test message.
  • "Heard repeats" after you send means a repeater picked you up.

Hear nothing? Recheck step 3, move the radio to a window or somewhere higher, then ask on Discord where the nearest repeater is. Your radio only needs to reach one.

Leave regions and scopes alone as a new user: the defaults reach everyone, including across state lines. If you later run a repeater, see repeaters.

Other ways to go off-grid in Indiana

All of these are in use around the state. Each project's own site is the place to learn it.

MeshtasticLoRa text mesh where every node can relay. No license. Start at meshtastic.org. GMRSLicensed UHF voice radio with repeaters. One family license, no exam. Rules and licensing at the FCC. Ham radioLicensed amateur radio, voice and data. Indiana clubs set local practice; getting licensed starts at the ARRL. ReticulumEncrypted networking over LoRa, Wi-Fi, packet radio, or the internet.
